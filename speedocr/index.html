SpeedOCR Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2025

On-Device OCR · No Cloud · No Tracking

Overview

SpeedOCR Studio is a privacy-focused OCR tool. All text recognition happens on your Mac. Your screen captures, documents, and extracted text never leave your device.

Data We Access

  • Screen Recording: SpeedOCR captures screen regions you select for OCR text extraction. Captures are temporary and processed immediately.
  • Camera: Used for document scanning. Camera input is processed on-device.
  • Microphone: Used for audio-to-text transcription features. Audio is processed locally.
  • Files: You can open and export documents. File access is user-initiated only.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No cloud servers. No telemetry. No advertising SDKs.
  • No account required. No login. No email.

On-Device Processing

All OCR and text recognition runs locally using macOS Vision framework. No images or text are sent to any external server.

Data Storage

  • Extracted text is stored locally and can be exported by you.
  • No persistent storage of screen captures — they are discarded after OCR processing.
  • You can clear all history from the app at any time.

In-App Purchases

SpeedOCR Studio offers Pro subscriptions (monthly, yearly, lifetime) via Apple's StoreKit. Payment is processed by Apple. We do not store or process any payment information.

Children's Privacy

SpeedOCR Studio is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

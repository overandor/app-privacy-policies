ClientContinuity — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2025

Overview

ClientContinuity is an AI-powered client relationship management tool. All client data is stored locally on your Mac. Your contacts, communications, and AI analysis stay on your device.

Data We Access

  • Contacts: ClientContinuity accesses your Contacts to manage client relationships. Contact data is stored and used locally.
  • Microphone: Used for AI secretary voice features. Audio is processed locally.
  • Speech Recognition: Used for voice-driven client management. Recognition happens on-device.
  • Files: CSV import/export for client data. File access is user-initiated only.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No cloud servers. No telemetry. No advertising SDKs.
  • No account required. No login. No email.

Local AI Processing

ClientContinuity uses on-device AI for the Cortex autopilot, behavioral analysis, and automated messaging suggestions. All AI processing happens locally on your Mac.

Data Storage

  • Client data is stored in a local database on your Mac.
  • Communication logs and audit trails are stored locally.
  • You can export or delete all data at any time from within the app.

In-App Purchases

ClientContinuity offers tiered subscriptions (Starter, Pro, Business) via Apple's StoreKit, in monthly and yearly options. Payment is processed by Apple. We do not store or process any payment information.

Children's Privacy

ClientContinuity is a business tool not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.
